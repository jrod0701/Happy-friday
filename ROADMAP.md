# Happy Friday roadmap

## Lynchpin games (next up, must be amazing)
Every round deals: **Pond Runner + Mail Man + one rotating game** from the pool (the third slot rotates by seed, as now).

### Pond Runner (auto runner, Temple Run style)
- Running through a helicopter factory floor (generic, no real brand names/logos). Hot job to get to: a clock/boss waiting at the end of the lane.
- Auto-run, speed ramps up. Jump over (crates, tool carts, floor hazards, cable reels), slide under (low pipes, hanging rotor blades, gantry beams), maybe lane-swap or a double-jump later.
- Collect quarters the union steward dropped (coin trails, combos). Score = distance + quarters.
- Seeded course so all three players get the same obstacles. 4 themes (factory floor, hangar night, paint shop, test pad). Fair but relentless, readable telegraphs, one-thumb controls (tap = jump, swipe/hold down = slide).
- Characters original; the runner wears the player's skin colours.

### Mail Man (Paperboy rip-off)
- Side-scrolling/isometric suburban street, deliver mail to mailboxes and doorsteps while dodging dogs, kids, cars, sprinklers, trash cans, lawn hazards.
- Throw mail left/right with touch; non-subscribers' windows are bonus breakables. Bike or walking route with speed lanes. Training course/bonus round feel at the end.
- Seeded street (same for all three), 4 themes (summer, fall, snow, night), a clear score of deliveries + bonus. Generic mail-carrier look, no real postal logos.
